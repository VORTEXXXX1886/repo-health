Code of Conduct
Our commitment
We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Expected behavior
Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
Give and accept feedback gracefully.
Focus on what is best for the community.
Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing another person's private information without permission, and unwelcome sexual attention.

Scope and enforcement
This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces. Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently exclude people for harmful behavior.

To report a concern, contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub.
